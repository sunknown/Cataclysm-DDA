# Disconnected avatars stay in the world as parked bodies

A lost client no longer ends the session. The seat becomes **disconnected**: its
avatar remains where it stood as an ordinary live body, with no AI and no
invulnerability, the barrier stops waiting for it, and its moves are forfeited.
Reconnecting takes control of that same avatar. There is no grace timer and no
conversion to a follower NPC in phase 5, deviating from §8.3 of the parent
design; the cheaper parked-body rule keeps one control path and lets the party
play on.
